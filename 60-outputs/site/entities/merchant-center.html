Glossary · Merchant Center

Google's tool through which merchants send product data feeds for Shopping ads, Search ads and organic results. Products need it to appear in the Shopping tab; product rich results do not.

Press and analysis 6

AnalysisD2-C508

For time-limited sales, give the sale price its validity dates in the product markup (Google's merchant listing documentation describes validFrom, validThrough and priceValidUntil) instead of editing markup by hand when the sale ends, and keep the dates aligned with the Merchant Center feed.

Ibrahim Anjro · Day 2 · What is Structured Data and why we need it on the internet.

AnalysisD3-C348

Google's free listings help says crawled store products can appear on Google without markup or Merchant Center but does not say whether that includes the Shopping tab; for a shop that wants Shopping tab visibility, the stage rule that Merchant Center is required is the safe working assumption.

Ibrahim Anjro · Day 3 · How Search results are born

  • Google Shopping
AnalysisD3-C351

For an online shop, Product structured data is enough for product rich results and Google Images product annotations, but the Shopping tab needs a Merchant Center feed too; keep feed and markup values identical, since Google can use the markup to validate the feed.

AnalysisD3-C366

Google's January 2026 announcement spoke of 'dozens' of new Merchant Center attributes, while the Day 3 talk counted six; all six named on stage (question_and_answer, document_link, related_product, item_group_title, variant_option, popularity_rank) are in the Merchant Center product data specification as of 3 October 2026.

Ibrahim Anjro · Day 3 · Shopping on Search: Beyond the blue links

AnalysisD3-C381

As of 3 October 2026 Google's Merchant Center help lists no schema.org property for question_and_answer, document_link, related_product, popularity_rank, variant_option or product_detail (only item_group_title maps, to ProductGroup.name), and Search Central does not document such markup: reading these attributes from schema.org is a stage statement, the feed is the documented route.

AnalysisD3-C410

Shop developers should first keep core feed and markup values (price, availability, brand, GTIN) accurate, then add Q&As, product highlights, specs as key-value product details and related products in the Merchant Center feed, and mirror them in schema.org Product markup only as Google documents support for it.
