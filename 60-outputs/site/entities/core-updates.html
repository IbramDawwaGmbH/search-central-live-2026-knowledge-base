Glossary · Core update

A significant, broad change to Google's ranking systems, made several times a year and announced on the Google Search Status Dashboard. Core updates do not target specific sites and are not penalties.

AnalysisD3-C299

After a core update drop, plan a site-wide quality review instead of page-by-page fixes or a technical 'recovery': have someone outside the team assess the site, compare it with the pages that gained, and test whether users can finish their task, for example with short satisfaction surveys.

Ibrahim Anjro · Day 3 · What are quality updates

AnalysisD3-C664

Set expectations for recovery work: a manual action can lift within weeks of a real clean-up, but recovery from a core update typically takes 3 to 6 months and may need the next core update, so do not judge quality work within the first weeks.
