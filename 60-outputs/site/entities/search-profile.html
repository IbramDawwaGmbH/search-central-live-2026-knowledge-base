Glossary · Search profile

A profile publishers and creators can claim in Google Search, with name, photo, bio, links and pinned posts, which users can follow. In the US, 10,000 followers across YouTube, Instagram, X or TikTok qualify since 16 September 2026. Its verified accounts are added automatically as Search Console platform properties.
