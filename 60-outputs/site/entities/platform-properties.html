Glossary · Platform properties

Search Console properties for Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube accounts, verified through a pop-up from the platform, that show how the account's content performs on Google Search, Discover and Google News.

Google’s documentation 3

Documented in

  • See how content from social and video platforms performs on Google Search Search Central blog (7 July 2026) · checked 3 October 2026

DocsSourceD3-C466

Google's help page on platform properties says ownership is checked periodically and, if the external login expires, access pauses until the account is re-verified; each account or channel needs its own property, and data takes a few days to appear.
