Glossary · max-snippet

A robots rule that caps a snippet at a number of characters: 0 means no snippet and -1 means no limit. It also limits how much of the page AI features may use.

Google’s documentation 2

Documented in

  • Robots meta tag, data-nosnippet, and X-Robots-Tag specifications Google Search Central · checked 3 October 2026
DocsSourceD2-C086

Google's robots meta tag specification says Google chooses the snippet length when no max-snippet rule is set, and with max-snippet:-1 chooses the length it believes most effective; it does not say that -1 produces longer snippets.

Google Search Central · Day 2 · Controlling indexing

DocsSourceD2-C728

Google's robots meta tag specification says the nosnippet rule also prevents a page's content from being used as a direct input for AI Overviews and AI Mode, and max-snippet also limits how much of it may be used that way.

Press and analysis 3

AnalysisD2-C075

Before adding nosnippet or a low max-snippet value, weigh the cost in AI features: both limit how much of the page AI Overviews and AI Mode can use, and John Mueller described those answers as built from snippets.

AnalysisD2-C105

Set max-snippet:-1 and max-image-preview:large on every indexable template unless licensing requires otherwise, and check that no CMS, plug-in or CDN setting adds lower snippet or image preview limits by default.

AnalysisD2-C731

Treat nosnippet, data-nosnippet and max-snippet as AI visibility settings too: Google lists them as the controls for content in AI features, and if AI answers are built from index snippets, as Google said on stage, a blocked or shortened snippet leaves AI Overviews and AI Mode less to use.
