Glossary · AI Mode

Google Search's AI mode, where users ask longer questions (about three times as long as a traditional search on average, a Google slide said; the opening keynote said two to three times) and follow-up questions. It uses the same crawling and index as Search and adds grounding and query fan-out when it answers.
