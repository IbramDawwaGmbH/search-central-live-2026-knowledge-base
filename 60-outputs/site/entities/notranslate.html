Thing · Directive

notranslate

A meta tag value and HTML class that stops Google from offering a translation of a page or of part of it.

DocsSourceD2-C095

Google's Search documentation gives notranslate as a rule for a robots or googlebot meta tag or an X-Robots-Tag header, and says the rule opts a page out of all translation features in Google Search.

AnalysisD2-C098

International sites should remove notranslate from their templates unless translation must be prevented: the robots or googlebot form switches off Google Search's translation features, and John Mueller said the form named google also blocks Chrome's translation for visitors who do not read the language.
