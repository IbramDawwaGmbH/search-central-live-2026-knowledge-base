Linked subscriptions

Users can link their paid subscriptions to Google so answers highlight paywalled content they already have access to. Google cited 34% more engagement among one publisher's linked subscribers. On Day 3 a Google speaker, unsure of the name, suggested linked subscriptions as an example of a search feature whose container holds text results (not in Google's docs).
