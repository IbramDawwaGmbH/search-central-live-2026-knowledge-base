Preferred sources

Users can mark sites as preferred sources. Those sites become more visible in Top Stories and are labelled in AI Mode and AI Overviews. Author’s view: it rewards brands people actively choose. On Day 3 Google said it can add a preferred-source badge, or a 'highly cited' badge, to a text result.
