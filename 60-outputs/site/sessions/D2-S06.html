Treat the 10,000-pixel viewport as a safety margin, not a target: load lazy content when it enters the viewport (Intersection Observer or native lazy loading, never scroll listeners), and give infinite-scroll content paginated URLs linked with <a href> so anything below the first rendered screen stays reachable.
