AnalysisD1-C532

A typo in a rule name is not always ignored by Google: its open-source robots.txt parser deliberately accepts common misspellings of disallow (such as dissallow, dissalow and disalow) and of user-agent (useragent, user agent), but not of allow. Google's spec page does not mention typos, and other crawlers may be stricter, so spell rule names correctly.
