AnalysisD2-C655

A site serving a smaller country in a big language, such as British English or Swiss German, should make that country unmistakable (country-code domain or region-specific hreflang, local address, prices and currency) so its pages can benefit from index selection's country balancing instead of competing with the much larger US or German content pool.
